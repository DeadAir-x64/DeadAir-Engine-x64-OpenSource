## 1. lizzardman - Headgear Animations, FDDA Redone

Профиль: https://www.moddb.com/members/lizzardman
Просим: анимации шоколадки и батончика + надевание шлемов и костюмов.

**Subject:** Permission request - Dead Air x64 port (free, non-commercial)

Hello,

I am part of a small team maintaining a 64-bit port of the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. mod Dead Air, built on the open-source OpenXRay engine. The port is free, non-commercial and published openly: https://github.com/DeadAir-x64/DeadAir-Engine-x64-OpenSource

We would like to ask your permission to include two things from your work:

1. The candy bar and chocolate bar eating animations from Headgear Animations (originally made by the MFS team, as your description notes).
2. The headgear and outfit animations from Headgear Animations - Dead Air has no animations for putting on helmets or suits at all, so this would be a real addition for our players.

Both addons are marked "Proprietary" on ModDB, which is why we are asking first rather than assuming. We have already gone this route with the author of Dead Air himself and with the authors of the other addons we use (FDDA, Ledge Grabbing, Mirrowel's food and drinks animations), and we credit every one of them.

If you agree, we would:

* credit you and the MFS team in the mod's readme, in the release notes and in the configuration files themselves, next to the animations;
* link back to your ModDB pages;
* change nothing about the animations themselves beyond what the different engine requires (our hands skeleton comes from Call of Chernobyl, so section offsets may need adjusting);
* respect any conditions you want to set, including "no" - in that case we simply will not use them.

Thank you for your work either way. The addons are excellent, and our players have been asking for exactly these animations.

Best regards,
DanesCrail
